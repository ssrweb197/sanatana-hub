1. Introduction

Welcome to Sanatana Hub ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information collected when you visit https://sanatanahub.com or interact with our digital offerings, interactive quizzes, books, and media.

By accessing or using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect two basic types of information when you interact with Sanatana Hub:

  • Personal Information Provided Voluntarily: When you submit a contact form, subscribe to updates, or participate in interactive sessions, we may collect information such as your full name, email address, and subject/message details.
  • Automatically Collected Non-Personal Data: Technical information automatically logged when visiting our platform, including IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, language preferences, referring URLs, and interaction patterns with our site pages.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly for purpose-driven operations, including:

  • Operating, maintaining, and enhancing the Sanatana Hub user experience.
  • Responding directly to user inquiries, support requests, and feedback.
  • Personalizing content delivery, theme preferences, and quiz performance metrics.
  • Monitoring technical performance, preventing fraudulent activity, and ensuring system security.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies Policy

Sanatana Hub utilizes small text files placed on your device known as "Cookies" to improve platform functionality and remember your preference choices (such as Dark/Light mode selections).

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies in your browser settings. However, doing so may affect how you are able to interact with our site and other websites.

5. Google AdSense

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on users' visits to Sanatana Hub and other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. You may also visit aboutads.info to opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising.

6. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze web traffic and understand visitor usage patterns. Google Analytics collects aggregate, non-personally identifiable information regarding visitor demographics and behavior on our site.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external websites, social platforms, and reference materials. Sanatana Hub has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites.

We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit via an external link from our platform.

8. Data Security

We implement standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. SSL encryption protocols are enforced site-wide.

While we take rigorous precautions, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is especially important to us. Although our Kids Corner features moral and educational stories designed for family audiences, we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

10. User Rights

Depending on your location, you hold specific rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data stored with us.

11. Updates to This Policy

Sanatana Hub reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact us:
