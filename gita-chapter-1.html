Arjuna Viṣāda Yoga

The Yoga of Arjuna's Despair

Chapter Overview

The first chapter sets the dramatic backdrop on the holy battlefield of Kurukshetra. As two colossal armies assemble for war, King Dhritarashtra asks his charioteer Sanjaya to describe the events taking place. Upon inspecting the enemy ranks, the mighty Pandava warrior Arjuna sees his own revered elders, teachers, cousins, and lifelong friends prepared to fight to the death.

Overcome by immense grief, attachment, and moral confusion, Arjuna drops his bow, Gandiva, and refuses to fight. His sorrow becomes the essential catalyst for Lord Krishna to deliver the eternal divine wisdom of the Gita.

Key Verses

Essential Shlokas

Pivotal verses capturing the opening dialogue and Arjuna's existential predicament.

VERSE 1 Dhritarashtra's Query

धृतराष्ट्र उवाच |
धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः ।
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय ॥ १-१ ॥

English Translation

Dhritarashtra said: O Sanjaya, assembled on the sacred plain of Kurukshetra, eager for battle, what did my sons and the sons of Pandu do?

VERSE 28 Arjuna's Grief

अर्जुन उवाच |
दृष्ट्वेमं स्वजनं कृष्ण युयुत्सुं समुपस्थितम् ।
सीदन्ति मम गात्राणि मुखं च परिशुष्यति ॥ १-२८ ॥

English Translation

Arjuna said: O Krishna, seeing my own kinsmen gathered here eager to fight, my limbs give way, my mouth dries up, and my body trembles.

VERSE 47 Arjuna Despairs

सञ्जय उवाच |
एवमुक्त्वार्जुनः सङ्ख्ये रथोपस्थ उपाविशत् ।
विसृज्य सशरं चापं शोकसंविग्नमानसः ॥ १-४७ ॥

English Translation

Sanjaya said: Having spoken thus on the battlefield, Arjuna cast aside his bow and arrows and sat down in the chariot, his mind overwhelmed with sorrow.
