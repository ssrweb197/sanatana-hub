Frequently Asked Questions

Meditation Guidance

How long should I meditate each day?
For beginners, starting with just 5 to 10 minutes daily is ideal. Consistency is far more effective than duration when establishing a mindfulness habit.
What should I do if my mind wanders while meditating?
Mind-wandering is entirely natural. Whenever you notice a thought, gently acknowledge it without judgment and bring your focus back to your breath or chant.
What is the difference between Pranayama and Meditation?
Pranayama involves conscious regulation of the breath to balance life force energy (Prana), whereas Meditation (Dhyana) is the continuous flow of single-pointed awareness.
