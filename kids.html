Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all quizzes on Sanatana Hub free to play?
Yes, all quizzes across all categories and difficulty levels are completely free for all visitors.
How often are new quizzes added?
We publish new daily challenge quizzes every 24 hours, along with weekly featured sets covering specific Puranas and historical temples.
Do I need to create an account to attempt a quiz?
No account is required to take quizzes, but logging in allows you to save your high scores and rank on the global leaderboard.
Are explanations provided after answering a question?
Yes, every question provides immediate, authentic context and scriptural references upon submission to deepen your understanding.
Can these quizzes be used for school or kids group learning?
Absolutely! Our Kids Quiz category is designed specifically for young minds, ideal for home learning or cultural workshops.
How is the community leaderboard score calculated?
Scores are calculated based on accuracy, difficulty multiplier, and completion speed across all attempted quiz sessions.
