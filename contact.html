Frequently Asked Questions

How can I contact Sanatana Hub?

You can fill out the contact form above or email us directly at contact@sanatanahub.com. Our team responds promptly.

How do I report an issue?

Select 'Report an Issue' in the form category dropdown and describe the problem along with page details so we can fix it.

How can I suggest a new article?

Select 'General Inquiry' or 'Feedback' and share topic suggestions, preferred scriptures, or authors you would like to see covered.

How can I publish with Sanatana Hub?

Choose 'Partnership' category with a brief summary of your work or research. Our editorial team will review submission options.

How do I access books?

Visit our dedicated Books section from the main navigation to read or download digital PDFs and study guides.

Are resources free?

Yes, all articles, stotras, meditation guides, kids content, and quizzes on Sanatana Hub are completely free for everyone.

Can I collaborate?

We welcome collaborations with scholars, content creators, artists, and educators. Send a proposal via 'Partnership' above.

How quickly do you respond?

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24 to 48 hours during business days.
